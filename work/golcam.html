Golcam

Automated goal capture, branding and WhatsApp delivery for a five-a-side football venue.

RoleProduct & Creative Direction
Status● Live, in production
ClientCentro Gol, Guadalajara
StackPython · Flask · Hikvision DVR · WhatsApp
Started2025

Centro Gol operates a five-a-side pitch that records every match on a security DVR. Golcam converts that raw footage into a branded, shareable clip: each goal is captured, watermarked and delivered directly to the group chat automatically, with no manual editing or export step.

I built the product end to end: a referee-facing app that triggers capture in real time, a Python and Flask server that pulls the clip from the Hikvision DVR, and a branding pipeline that applies a custom watermark sequence before delivery through WhatsApp. I also directed the visual language for the on-court scoreboard, a high-contrast, brutalist system built around the venue's institutional palette of signal green, alert red and near-black, designed for legibility from across the pitch in direct sunlight.

The clip pipeline has run in production at Centro Gol since 2025. A companion live scoreboard and referee application, tracking full match state, fouls and cards over a real-time WebSocket connection between the referee's phone and the courtside monitor, is in active development as the next stage of the system.

The project combines product design, backend development and brand systems work in a single pipeline that runs unattended for every match, and reflects the end-to-end ownership, from concept through production infrastructure, that defines my creative technology practice.
