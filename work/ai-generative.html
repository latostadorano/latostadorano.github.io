AI & Generative

An ongoing experimental series in Midjourney, Stable Diffusion and Runway.

RoleExperimental / Image Generation
ClientPersonal
Year2024–Ongoing

This ongoing series is where I develop and test practical AI image-generation skills: prompt engineering, image coherence, and the visual judgment needed to push a generated aesthetic without it breaking down. Tools include Midjourney, Stable Diffusion and Runway, with Claude increasingly handling the workflow layer that connects them.

The work also functions as applied practice in AI visual evaluation: assessing lighting, anatomy, composition and narrative alignment in generated imagery, and distinguishing output that holds up under sustained scrutiny from output that only reads well at a glance. This evaluation skill set applies directly to production use cases such as AI-assisted content review and generative asset quality control.
