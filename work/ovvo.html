OvvO

Generative GIFs built with p5.js for an automated Twitter bot.

RoleVisual Identity & Motion
ClientPersonal / @ovvo_bot
Year2024

OvvO started as a question: what happens when a visual identity isn't a fixed logo but a living system that generates its own variations, forever? I built a series of generative GIFs in p5.js and connected them to @ovvo_bot, a Twitter bot that posted new generative art automatically, no manual curation, no repeat.

Each piece is a small closed system: a set of rules, a palette, and enough randomness that no two outputs are quite the same. It's an ongoing collection, and the earliest sketch that eventually pointed me toward the automation work I do today: the moment design stopped being only about the final frame and started being about the system that produces the frame.
