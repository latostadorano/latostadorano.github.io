Bike Punk

A flyer series for a self-organized nocturnal bike ride, designed without a client-approval chain.

RolePrint / Flyer Design
ClientBike Punks (community collective)
Year2024

Bike Punks is a self-organized nocturnal bike ride with no institutional sponsor. I designed a flyer series to promote each ride, working directly with the organizing collective without a formal client sign-off process, using Illustrator and Photoshop for concept development and print-ready production.

Each flyer was scoped independently based on that week's ride theme, giving the series visual range while maintaining a consistent underground print aesthetic across the full run. The project is a practical exercise in fast-turnaround print design and maintaining brand cohesion without a fixed style guide.
