Katfish

Visual direction for a music video, built around mood rather than illustration.

RoleVisual Direction
ClientKatfish (independent artist)
Year2024
WatchYouTube ↗

Katfish came to me with a finished track and no visual language yet. Rather than illustrate the lyrics literally, I built the video's look around the track's mood: frame composition, color treatment and motion designed to feel like the song, not narrate it.

The frames lean into texture and contrast, generative motion elements mixed with directed footage, so the video reads as one continuous atmosphere rather than a sequence of separate scenes cut to the beat.
