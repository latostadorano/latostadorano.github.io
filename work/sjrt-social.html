SJRT Network

Editorial and campaign design across TV, radio and news — at production speed.

RoleMultimedia Designer
StatusOngoing
ClientSJRT — Jalisco TV, Radio & News
Started2025

SJRT runs three simultaneous public channels — JaliscoTV, JaliscoRADIO and JaliscoNOTICIAS — each needing daily, on-brand visual content at a pace most design teams would call unreasonable. My job is to keep every piece visually sharp and relevant while staying inside strict brand guidelines: commemorative cards, cultural graphics and branded posts, produced and published the same day.

In summer 2025 that same system scaled up for a specific push: a vertical social campaign announcing SJRT's summer programs, with a consistent typography, color and layout system carried across every asset — designed and delivered under a tight production window.
