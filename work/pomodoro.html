Pomodoro

A gentle multi-timer for people who lose track of time.

RoleProduct Design & Build
StatusLive · web & macOS
ClientPersonal product
StackWeb app · Electron (macOS)
→ Open the web app → Download for Mac (.dmg)

There's a version of you that knows exactly what time it is. You're probably not that version right now, and that's fine, it's the whole reason this exists. Pomodoro sits on your screen and watches the time so you don't have to, while you go somewhere inside a problem, a drawing or a sentence that isn't working yet.

It counts. It glows a little. You can run more than one timer at once, if you're the kind of person who's doing more than one thing at once, which you probably are. Each timer gets its own color and its own rhythm. There's a compact mode that collapses everything into a thin strip of light at the edge of the screen: you barely see it, but you know it's there, and that's enough.

I designed and built it end to end, then shipped it twice: as a browser app with nothing to install, and as a native macOS app packaged with Electron for people who want it living permanently in their dock. Same product, same interaction model, two very different distribution problems to solve.
