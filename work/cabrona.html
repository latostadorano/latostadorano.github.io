Cabrona

Brand identity and product content for an independent accessories label.

RoleBranding & Product Content
ClientCabrona
Year2024
TypeFreelance

Cabrona needed product content that could stand next to any hospitality or fashion account and not blink: bold type, minimal staging, zero compromise. I handled brand identity and product content end to end: still photography direction and short video loops built specifically for Instagram's feed and story formats.

The brief was simple and unforgiving: make the product the only thing anyone looks at. No clutter, no props competing for attention, just clean staging and a typographic system confident enough to carry the brand on its own.
