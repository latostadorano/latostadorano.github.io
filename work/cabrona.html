Cabrona

Brand identity and an AI-generated content system, built from the brand's own product photography, for an independent accessories label.

RoleBranding & AI Content
ClientCabrona
Year2024
TypeFreelance

I managed brand identity and social content end to end for Cabrona, an independent accessories label. The brand supplied base product photography, and from those I generated still images and short video clips with AI, built specifically for Instagram's feed and story formats. The system needed to read as confidently as any established hospitality or fashion account, with bold type, minimal staging and consistent lighting across every generated asset.

The brief prioritized product clarity above all: clean staging, no competing props, and a typographic system strong enough to carry the brand without a logo in frame. Deliverables included a repeatable generation workflow the client could reuse for future product drops without outside creative direction.
