About

Pablo Toust · GDL, Mexico

Multimedia designer with over 15 years of experience in visual communication, branding, and digital content creation.

Currently at SJRT (Jalisco TV, Radio & News), designing for real audiences in real formats. Previously led creative teams at a digital marketing agency across branding, social media, and political campaigns.

My work blends graphic design, motion, generative AI tools, and creative coding. I use p5.js on a Tuesday and deliver an editorial billboard on Wednesday.

B.A. in Integral Design from ITESO. Technical Degree in Audiovisual Media from CAAV. Data Analytics at TripleTen. CS50 Python via Harvard.
